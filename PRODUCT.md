# LinkForge

Dashboard-first link management, bio landing page builder, smart routing, DeepLinks, A/B testing, MultiShield bot/traffic protection, Radar health checks, forms/quizzes, real-time analytics, and 19 creator/marketing utilities.

## Constraints
- Zero pricing, billing, plans, or upgrade prompts.
- Instant workspace access with pre-seeded demo data and multi-workspace/team role switching.
- Real local SQLite persistence (`node:sqlite`) and live `/[slug]` redirect + `/p/[slug]` bio page engine.
